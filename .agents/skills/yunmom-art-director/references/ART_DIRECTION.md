# Art Direction

## North star
YunMom should feel like a premium, warm, living cloud world that quietly takes care of pregnancy complexity without making the user feel managed.

## Priority stack
1. Gentle warmth
2. Very low psychological/visual pressure
3. Premium aesthetic quality
4. Airiness and vitality
5. Brand consistency
6. Cute without childishness
7. Richness without noise
8. Nonessential feature visibility last; Safety, consent, destructive confirmation, and accessibility remain explicit

## Visual language
- Short-pile plush 3D / 2.5D softness
- White cloud as the dominant living material
- High-lightness pale pink, sky blue, pearl white; lavender/coral/gold accents
- Clean, luminous, never grey-muddy
- Soft depth and tactile material; no cheap glossy plastic
- Spacious composition and minimal persistent text
- “华丽，但是安静”

## Emotional-load rules
- HOME must never look like a task dashboard.
- No red-dot culture, streaks, completion percentages, or guilt states.
- Silent Day is visually complete, not empty.
- Ordinary pregnancy tasks may use stars. Medical risk never uses dramatic character acting; a compact `!` may be only an entry affordance, while the controlled surface carries explicit R0–R3 meaning and action.

## Originality
The user-provided cloud-pillow image is an inspiration for softness/material/comfort, not a template to reproduce. Create an original YunMom IP silhouette, face, nest, and interaction language.

## When taste is unspecified
Choose the version that would remain pleasant after hundreds of daily opens, not the version that is merely most striking in a one-off concept image.
