# Approved Invariants

These are contract-aligned creative invariants. They may change through a new aesthetic-Golden decision, but any item touching a frozen product or professional domain also requires the applicable written change and re-signatures. Contract change, domain-review failure, or invalid evidence makes the affected Golden stale even without new taste feedback.

- Product name: 云妈妈 / YunMom
- YunMom is a white large pillow-like cloud creature with a minimal face.
- Short-pile plush 3D/2.5D tactile visual language.
- No human body and no ordinary arms/hands; use Cloud Puff deformation.
- Baby uses the same visual universe and changes by gestational-stage art, not individual medical status.
- While the Episode state makes pregnancy progress appropriate, Pregnancy Time is above the active HOME character composition. After confirmed Gentle Closure/quiet archive it is N/A and must disappear; Baby visibility becomes an explicit user choice.
- No Bottom Tab; HOME spatial navigation remains as product baseline.
- High-lightness pale pink + pale blue + white; never make grey/muddy tones the dominant palette.
- Ordinary tasks are soft 3D stars.
- Silent Day is a complete, beautiful state with no stars or task-pressure UI.
- Characters do not act distressed to communicate medical risk. A compact calm `!` may be explored as a HOME entry affordance, but its symbol, size, color, and position are not frozen and never carry the complete Safety meaning.
- Every Safety surface preserves R0–R3 semantics with explicit severity, what happened, what to do now, a non-decorative icon, correct accessibility role/label/state, and a reachable next action. `dismiss` never means `resolve`.
- Persistent AI writes expose the correct Receipt and correction/undo/delete controls for the actual Command class; a generic trash icon never implies identical deletion semantics.
- UI is extremely minimal with text appearing mainly when contextually needed.
- iOS and Android share one P0 accessibility contract: screen readers, maximum accessibility text/at least 200%, non-gesture alternatives, qualified contrast, focus, and system Reduce Motion.
- Widget is default-off, per-field opt-in, read-only, low sensitivity, never a Safety channel, and cleared on withdrawal, Gentle Closure, Episode transition/deletion, or delete-all.
- Episode UI supports `1..N BabySubject`; unknown attribution stays explicit and layouts never silently collapse multiple subjects into one.
- Versioned machine-readable semantic Design Tokens are the sole implementation Token source; decoration cannot override Safety or accessibility.
- Overall rule: “华丽，但是安静”.
