# Local research corpus

Files in this directory are local-only research inputs. They are excluded from
Git by default because their redistribution rights and clinical authority have
not been established.

They must not be used as an approved Medical Safety Rule Pack, Clinical Golden,
or production RAG source until all of the following exist:

1. a lawful-use and redistribution record;
2. a versioned source identity and checksum;
3. a declared jurisdiction, population, gestational scope, and review date;
4. qualified MD review and the required LEGAL approval;
5. a synthetic/de-identified evaluation corpus and regression evidence.

Do not place real patient records, reports, names, hospital details, account
identifiers, prompts, responses, or other sensitive health content here.
